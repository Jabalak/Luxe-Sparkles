Our Story
Born from a love of beautiful things

Luxe Sparkles began in 2024 in the heart of Mauritius — a tiny island with a big love for beauty. Our founder, inspired by the shimmer of the Indian Ocean and the golden sunsets of Grand Port, set out to create jewellery that felt luxurious yet deeply personal.

We source only the finest 925 Sterling Silver, 24K Gold Plated, and premium Stainless Steel, working with artisans who treat each piece as a labour of love — not a product on a line.

Sparkling Facts
The History Behind the Shine
🐚
The First Jewellery

The oldest known jewellery is 130,000 years old — eagle talons found in Croatia, believed to have been worn as a necklace by Neanderthals. Beauty truly is timeless.

⚗️
Gold Never Tarnishes

Gold is so chemically stable that every gram of gold ever mined still exists today. The ring on your finger may contain atoms that once adorned an ancient Egyptian queen.

💎
Diamonds from Stars

Scientists have discovered a star 50 light-years away that is essentially a crystallised diamond — 10 billion trillion trillion carats. The universe itself loves sparkle.

🌊
Silver from the Sea

The ocean holds an estimated 20 million tonnes of dissolved gold and silver. Our 925 Sterling Silver is among the purest alloys in modern jewellery-making — 92.5% pure silver.

🌙
Moonstone Magic

Romans believed moonstone was formed from solidified moonbeams. Its ethereal glow — called adularescence — is caused by light scattering between microscopic layers of feldspar.

🐍
The Snake Necklace

Serpent jewellery dates to ancient Egypt & Rome as symbols of eternal love and protection. Cleopatra herself was rarely seen without a serpent armband — our Serpent Chain carries that legacy.
